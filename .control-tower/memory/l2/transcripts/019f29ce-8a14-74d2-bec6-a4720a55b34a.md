## User

Explain the cone angle and attenuation radio etc

## Agent

For your flashlight, these are Unreal SpotLight controls:

`--inner-cone-angle 8`
Inside this angle, the light is at full strength. Smaller means a tighter bright center.

`--outer-cone-angle 20`
Outside this angle, the light is zero. Between inner and outer, it fades off. Bigger gap means softer feathering at the edge.

So `8 / 20` means: narrow bright core, with a fade from 8 degrees to 20 degrees.

`--attenuation-radius 450`
Maximum distance the light reaches, in Unreal units. Unreal uses centimeters, so `450` is about `4.5 m`. Larger radius reaches farther but can brighten more of the room and cost more.

`--intensity 1500`
Brightness of the spotlight. Higher makes illuminated surfaces brighter. If radius is large, the same intensity can affect more visible area.

`--indirect-lighting-intensity 0`
How much this light contributes to bounced/global illumination. `0` means no indirect bounce from the flashlight, which is usually better for controlled active-light experiments.

`--scene-light-intensity-scale 0.0005`
Scales existing scene lights down. `0.0005` is almost off, but not exactly off. Use `0` if you want no scene-light contribution.

Practical tuning:

```text
Narrow beam:     inner 4-8,   outer 12-25
Soft flashlight: inner 2-8,   outer 45-70
Short range:     attenuation 250-500
Room range:      attenuation 800-1500
```

Your current settings are a tight, short-range beam:

```text
inner 8
outer 20
attenuation 450 cm
intensity 1500
```
